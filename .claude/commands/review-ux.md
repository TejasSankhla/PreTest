Quick UX audit for: **$ARGUMENTS**

## Your job:

Fast validation check (2-3 min) focusing on:

1. **First Impression** - What feeling does this evoke in 3 seconds?
2. **Visual Hierarchy** - What draws the eye first? Is that right?
3. **Psychology** - Does this calm or stress a nervous student?
4. **Modern Design** - Bento grids, gradients, micro-interactions used well?
5. **Mobile Reality** - Works at 375px? Touch targets 44px+?
6. **Trust Signals** - Credentials, social proof visible?
7. **Brand Tone** - Does copy feel supportive, not sales-y?

## Output format:

**First Impression:**
[1 sentence - what do I feel when I see this?]

**✅ What's Working:**
- [Good thing 1]
- [Good thing 2]

**🚨 Critical Issue:**
[ONE thing that kills the experience]
→ **Fix:** [specific suggestion]

**⚡ Quick Wins:**
- [Easy fix 1]
- [Easy fix 2]

**Mobile Check:**
[Will this work on a phone? Any concerns?]

**Questions for you:**
- [Anything I need clarification on]

Keep it fast and actionable. Focus on psychology + modern design + attraction.
